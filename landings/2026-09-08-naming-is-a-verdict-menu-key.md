# Naming moves to the verdict menu

`space a n` named the agent under the point. `tab` then `n` names the card
in view. It is the same prompt writing the same `Property::Name` on the
agent's own cell; what changed is the subject, and that is the whole point:
a name is the user's own words about the thing they are looking at, and the
agent under the point is not always the card on screen.

This is the rule from the done deletion applied once more — a verdict has
one door, the verdict menu; nothing that acts on a card lives anywhere else.
Naming is not a verdict, but it is an act on the card, and the reason to
keep it in one place is the same reason.

Over a card that is not an agent the key says so rather than doing
something; a Slack unit is the same name on its own cell and gets the key
once the desk mirror lands.
