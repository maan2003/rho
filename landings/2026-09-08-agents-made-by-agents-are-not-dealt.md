# An agent created by an agent belongs to its creator

The user's engineers spawn engineers of their own, and until now every one
of them was dealt, listed under running on Home, and offered by Find, as if
the user had made it. They are the creator's implementation detail: the
creator reads their work and answers their questions, and what the user
needs to know reaches them through the creator's own card.

The rule is applied on the creation fact, which never changes: an agent
with a creator is not the user's. Filing is placement and says nothing
about it. The dealer leaves such agents out of the facts it cards from, so
no filing walks down into what its agent spawned and no remake makes a
card for one; the running list and Find skip them the same way.

Per event nothing new is walked: the fact is one lookup per agent in the
passes that already ran over every agent, and the walk from a filed agent
down through its descendants is gone.
