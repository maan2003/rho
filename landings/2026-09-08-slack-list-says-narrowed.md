# The list says what it is narrowed to

*eng-bgkw, 2026-09-08.*

Searching the Slack list narrows it, and the narrowing outlives the prompt:
nothing clears it but another search. Until now nothing on screen said so.
A reader who searched an hour ago saw a list of four rows, with the rest of
Slack simply absent — no line about it, no mark, and the surface title is
the constant "slack". "nothing matches" appeared only when a query reached
zero rows; a query that reached some was invisible.

The list now carries one line above the rows while a query stands:

    matching "ops" · 4 of 27

The count is against the whole workspace, so it says how much is being kept
off the screen as well as that something is.

What changes for the user: a narrowed list says it is narrowed and by how
much. When the narrowing clears is unchanged — that is a design question,
not something to alter on the way past.
