# `tab` opens the verdicts

The verdict menu opened on a tap of `shift`. The user found the modifier
distracting, and the tap needed the platform's modifier events, a hold
timer to tell it from a chord, and a rule for the `shift` keystroke some
platforms deliver on its own. `tab` needs none of that.

`tab` over a card opens the verdicts; `tab` again is Home, which the menu's
own `tab` row says. Over a surface that is no card it goes Home in one
press, and from Home it is the way back. On the draft `tab` still walks
the fields, because the draft answers first. A menu or a prompt holding
the keyboard keeps the key.

The modifier listener, the two timestamps, the hold constant and the
keystroke rule are deleted with the tests that were only about them.
