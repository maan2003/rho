# A verdict has one door

The agent menu had a done. `space a d`, and `ctrl-shift-d` beside it, wrote
the same `DeskVerdict::Done` that `d` in the verdict menu writes — but over
a different subject. The menu's `d` acts on the card in view, which is what
the user is looking at; `space a d` acted on `self.subject(…).agents`, the
agents under the point. Two doors to one verdict, and the two disagreed
about what they were a verdict on.

That is the same shape as the snooze deleted earlier today, and it goes the
same way: `space a d`, `ctrl-shift-d`, the `AgentDone` action,
`Command::AgentDone` and `cmd_agent_done` are gone. The rule is written into
HOME-DESIGN: a verdict has one door, the verdict menu; nothing duplicates a
verdict elsewhere.

Nothing is lost. `tab` over the card and `d` is the done, and it is the
done that lands on what the user can see.
